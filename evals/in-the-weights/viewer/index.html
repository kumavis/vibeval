What earns credit?

Every answer has filled scoring inputs. A fixed rule computes its score; no model judge, keyword heuristic, or discretionary bonus is used. Evidence classification is the review step.

Name-only answers earn 2/2 for identifying a MetaMask founder or co-creator with no material biographical issue found; 1/2 if they add a contradicted or unsupported claim; 0/2 for no recognition. Abstention is distinct from an invented identity.

The MetaMask cue always earns zero unaided credit. False-premise corrections have their own score above. Mere mentions of MetaMask earn nothing.

“Unsupported” means the reviewed sources do not substantiate the claim, not that it has been proven false. The review covers material historical and biographical claims, not current job titles or every adjective.
